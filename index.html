TracReal

At TracReal, we’re building tools to help organisations detect manipulated media and trace the source of the content they share.

Detect
Analyse images, video and audio for signs of manipulation, with explanations to support review.
Protect
Embed invisible watermarks that carry a recoverable identifier, helping trace media back to its source.
