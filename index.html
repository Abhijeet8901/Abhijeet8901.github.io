Abhijeet Narang

Researcher at Monash University

I’m a researcher at Monash University working on efficient and explainable deepfake detection models. My research examines how well these models detect manipulation and whether their explanations match the visual evidence. I’m supervised by Assoc. Prof. Abhinav Dhall, Prof. Jianfei Cai (IEEE Fellow), and Assoc. Prof. Muhammad Haris Khan.

I’m also a co-founder of TracReal. Previously, I was a software engineer at Microsoft. I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad.
